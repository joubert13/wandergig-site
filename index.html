A marketplace in the making

Short, paid tasks for businesses — done by travelers already in the city.

WanderGig is a planned, mobile-first marketplace for location-specific micro-tasks: photos, local checks, quick research, and small hand-offs. It isn’t live yet.

The idea

How it would work

For businesses

Need a photo from a particular street, a quick check of a shop or venue, a bit of local research, or a small hand-off? WanderGig is meant to be a lighter option than sending a staffer or hiring a field-research firm for a one-off job.

For travelers

If you’re already in a city — backpacking, working remotely, or traveling for leisure — you could pick up a short paid task between plans. Not a full-time gig app, and not remote freelance work. Just something you can do, in about 20–30 minutes, while you’re already there.

We’re in early research

There is no product to sign up for, and I’m not selling anything. These 3-minute surveys will shape what gets built — including whether it gets built at all. A clear “this wouldn’t be useful” is just as helpful as a yes.
