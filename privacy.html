Privacy

WanderGig is in early research. This page explains how survey responses are handled.

Surveys

The traveler and business surveys linked from this site are hosted by Typeform. When you click a survey button, you leave wandergig.co and go to a form on typeform.com. Typeform’s own privacy policy also applies on that page.

How responses are used

I use survey responses only to research whether WanderGig would be useful, and to shape what (if anything) gets built. I do not sell responses, and I do not use them for advertising.

Asking for your data to be deleted

If you have submitted a survey and would like your responses deleted, email joubert@wandergig.co. I’ll delete them from the research records I control, and I’ll request deletion from Typeform where possible.
